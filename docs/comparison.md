# Site comparison — Build 5.8

Open `#/compare` or choose Compare sites. Select two to four distinct sites and one basis: recorded base, DBKL classification as read, or current live scenarios. Missing models stay unassessed. The comparison does not change the lab, owner, incentive or calibration settings.

## Reading the results
- RLV per acre normalizes scale. Total RLV, owner consideration, shortfall and surplus refer to the displayed test parcel only.
- Sites without mapped site area use the existing one-acre test parcel. Their assembled-site budget and buyer ticket are unknown.
- Recorded base excludes discretionary incentives. The guideline scenario uses the recorded redevelopment classification without residing, open-space or transit additions. Approval remains unresolved.
- Live scenarios may contain different presets or analyst edits. Inspect effective PR, ASP and area before comparing outcomes.
- Buyer candidates reuse the land-deal heuristic, with supporting deal links. Scores are neither probabilities nor expressions of interest.
- Evidence counts are inventories, not quality scores. Imported transactions remain provisional and require comparability review.
- No composite winner is calculated. Positive coverage does not resolve planning approval, legal assembly, owner consent or evidence gaps.

The JSON export records the selected basis, input snapshots, outputs and evidence gaps. Selection is session-only; existing scenario settings use the existing workspace persistence.
